How we began

Start with people.
Build from there.

RRNGO was born from a simple, powerful belief: people closest to a challenge should have a meaningful voice in shaping its solutions.

We began in Tamil Nadu in 2019, working with a small group of volunteers determined to make a difference in their own backyard. What started as informal community meetings grew into structured programmes touching thousands of lives.

Today, our four programmes — Education, Healthcare, Women Empowerment and Child Hunger — are guided by the communities they serve. Our role is to show up with respect, bring resources, and support the work already happening.

A message from our founder

"Real change begins when we listen to one another and make space for communities to lead."

When I started RRNGO, I wasn't looking to build an organisation. I was looking to build trust. Trust with neighbours, with families, with children who deserved more than what they had been given.

Every programme we run, every meal we serve, every woman we help stand on her own two feet — it all began with a conversation. It began by listening without judgement, and acting without hesitation.

I believe that every community holds its own solutions. Our job is simply to create the conditions for those solutions to flourish. If you share this belief, I invite you to walk alongside us.
